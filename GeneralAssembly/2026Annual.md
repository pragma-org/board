The Administrative Board provided a high-level overview of potential credential management
systems, including Bitwarden and 1Password.

### 10. Services To Be Provided By PRAGMA To Maintainer Committees

Lara Elliott, as one of PRAGMA's attorneys, presented the background for the proposed provision
of legal services to maintainer committee members.

The Administrative Board recommended:

- providing legal services to maintainer committee members through PRAGMA, particularly for
  contract negotiations or disputes with third-party service providers hired to perform work on
  maintainer committee projects;
- drafting formal regulations describing the conditions for providing such legal services;
- drafting formal regulations on the relationship between maintainer committees, maintainer
  committee members as individuals, PRAGMA and third-party service providers.

The background for this proposal arose from contract negotiations between Antithesis and the
Amaru maintainer committee members for software testing services. It became clear that
third-party service providers, particularly commercial and legal decision-makers, found PRAGMA's
governance structure, its relationship to maintainer committees and some aspects of Cardano
Treasury funding difficult to follow.

The concerns centered on:

- the identity of contractual counterparties;
- liability questions;
- potential involvement of PRAGMA in the contract relationship.

The Administrative Board explained that formal regulations would provide guidance for
maintainer committees, document PRAGMA's governance structure in more detail and
support commercial negotiations between maintainer committee members and third-party service
providers.

Matthias Benkort referred to the PRAGMA-Amaru Maintainer Committee framework agreement,
which was developed in 2025 by the Cardano Foundation legal team. This agreement could serve
as a basis for the regulations and as a template framework agreement for future maintainer
committees.

The Administrative Board asked the General Assembly to approve the provision of legal services
to maintainer committee members through PRAGMA, particularly for contract negotiations or
disputes with third-party service providers hired to perform work on maintainer committee
projects.

**Decision:** The General Assembly unanimously approved the provision of legal services to
maintainer committee members through PRAGMA, particularly for contract negotiations or
disputes with third-party service providers hired to perform work on maintainer committee
projects.

Based on article 22 paragraph 1 letter j of the PRAGMA Statutes, the Administrative Board
confirmed that it would draw up and issue the necessary regulations. The General Assembly
agreed with this proceeding.

### 11. Financial Matters

#### Financial Statement For 2025

The membership received PRAGMA's Financial Statement 2025. 

- tax duties arose due to services bought from German-domiciled Christian Grobmeier, and an
  accrual was made;
- past membership fees were invoiced with VAT included. Under Swiss law, this created a
  taxable event even though VAT was not owed on those transactions. The Administrative Board
  proposed retrospectively increasing the 2025 membership fees by the amount of VAT paid in
  error, then issuing new invoices to replace the old invoices. This would remedy the mistake
  without sending money back and forth.

Because the proposed correction resulted in an increase in the membership fee, General Assembly
approval was required.

**Decision:** The General Assembly unanimously approved the VAT correction.

The Administrative Board recommended approval of the Financial Statement 2025.

**Decision:** The General Assembly unanimously approved the Financial Statement 2025.

#### Budget And Membership Fee For 2026

The budget for 2026 and PRAGMA membership fee for 2026 were discussed together.

The membership received PRAGMA's proposed budget for 2026. The Administrative
Board explained that the higher costs on the intellectual property budget line for 2026 was
intended to give PRAGMA room to cover potential intellectual property costs, including trademark
disputes and potential new projects.

Blink Labs had been paying specific IT costs directly, including Google Workspace and
Cloudflare. It was noted that PRAGMA should pay or reimburse Blink Labs for these
costs. Blink Labs had no objection to continuing to pay them. Probst Partner suggested putting a
one-page governance note in place between PRAGMA and Blink Labs to confirm Blink Labs'
intent to pay these costs as a donation to PRAGMA and to document PRAGMA's direct use of the
resources.

Pi Lanningham requested monthly membership fee payments due to cash-flow concerns.

The members suggested paying the membership fee in monthly installments and
reassessing PRAGMA's financial needs for the final two quarters of 2026. If budgeted intellectual
property items were not needed based on actual projects received and the status of potential
disputes, the General Assembly could reassess the need for full payment of outstanding
membership-fee instalments.

**Decision:** The General Assembly unanimously approved the membership fee and budget for
2026, implementing:

- monthly instalment payments;
- reassessment in late 2026 of whether outstanding instalments must be paid if other budget
  items are not needed.
