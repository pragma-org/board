  The Administrative Board presents the activity report, appended to these Minutes as Appendix 1.

  ### a. Phase Two Update

  The General Assembly voted to initiate Phase Two per art. 4 par. 2 of the PRAGMA Statutes on 30 May 2025. The
  Administrative Board gives the General Assembly a report on activities undertaken since that date.

  ### b. Value Proposition Update

  In 2025 the General Assembly approved a value proposition for PRAGMA and now presents an update on the
  implementation of this value proposition and the status of the individual items:

  - **Open source mentorship:** setting up and managing a group of willing mentors that will coordinate with
  projects that require their insights. Implementation of this value proposition began in 2025.
  - **Field tested documentation and workflows:** consolidating in a single open source repository useful
  documentation for projects and breakdowns of workflows that can be helpful for open source projects.
  Implementation of this value proposition began in 2025.
  - **Assessment of maturity of an open source project:** creating easy to use checklists to assess the state of a
  project and recommend a set of steps to improve the maturity level of a project. In 2025 no headway was made with
  this value proposition.
  - **PRAGMA ecosystem recommendation:** building a set of existing open source solutions for specific use cases
  based on a set of criteria that will contribute to the project success. In 2025 no headway was made with this
  value proposition.

  ## 11. Vote on Choice of Services to be Provided by PRAGMA to the Maintainer Committees

  Lara Elliott, as one of PRAGMA's attorneys, is asked by Christina Gianelloni to present the background to this
  agenda item as set out below.

  The Administrative Board recommends:

  1. the provision of legal services to the maintainer committee members via PRAGMA, particularly for the
  negotiation of contracts or disputes with third-party service providers hired to perform work on the maintainer
  committee projects;
  2. the drafting of formal regulations describing the conditions for the provision of such legal services; and
  3. the drafting of formal regulations on the relationship between the maintainer committees, the maintainer
  committee members as individuals, PRAGMA, and third-party service providers.

  ### Background

  The Administrative Board noted that recent third-party service discussions involving a maintainer committee
  highlighted the need for clearer written guidance on PRAGMA's governance structure, the relationship between
  PRAGMA and maintainer committees, and the role of third-party service providers.

  The concerns identified included:

  - identity of contractual counterparties;
  - liability questions; and
  - potential involvement of PRAGMA in third-party service relationships.

  In order to facilitate future such contracts by addressing these concerns, the Administrative Board will draft
  regulations to be issued by the Administrative Board which expressly clarify these points.

  Such regulations would:

  - provide guidance for maintainer committees;
  - document PRAGMA Association's governance structure in more detail; and
  - be intended for dissemination to commercial and legal decision-makers with third-party service providers, in
  order to facilitate commercial negotiations between maintainer committee members and service providers.

  Against this background, the Administrative Board also recommends that the General Assembly should approve the
  provision of legal services to the maintainer committee members through PRAGMA, in accordance with art. 18 let. l
  of the PRAGMA Statutes. Such legal services would especially aim to support maintainer committee members in
  contract negotiations with third parties.

  If the General Assembly approves this point in principle, the Administrative Board would draw up regulations
  setting out the conditions for maintainer committee members to request such services from PRAGMA Association for
  individual projects, as well as potential models for financing such services.

  The Administrative Board noted that existing maintainer committee framework materials may serve as a basis for the
  regulations and as template framework materials for future maintainer committees.

  The Administrative Board therefore asks the General Assembly to approve the provision of legal services to the
  maintainer committee members through PRAGMA, particularly for the negotiation of contracts or in case of disputes
  with third-party service providers hired to perform work on the maintainer committee projects.

  **Decision:** The General Assembly unanimously approves the provision of legal services to the maintainer
  committee members through PRAGMA, particularly for the negotiation of contracts or disputes with third-party
  service providers hired to perform work on the maintainer committee projects.

  Based on art. 22 par. 1 let. j PRAGMA Statutes, the Administrative Board confirms that it will draw up and issue
  the necessary regulations. The General Assembly agrees with this proceeding.

  ## 12. Financial Matters

  ### a. Financial Statements for 2025

  The membership has received PRAGMA's Financial Statement 2025. Benjamin Bürgi leads the General Assembly through
  the financial statement for 2025 and presents clarifications on tax treatment, invoicing, accounting
  classification, and prior-year cost allocation.

  Past membership fees were invoiced with VAT included. Under Swiss law, this gave rise to a taxable event, even
  though VAT is not owed on these transactions. Consequently, the Administrative Board suggests retrospectively
  correcting the 2025 membership invoices to remedy the mistake without unnecessary money movement. Because this
  results in an adjustment to the membership fee treatment, General Assembly approval is required.

  **Decision:** The General Assembly unanimously approves this correction.

  The Administrative Board also clarifies that one cost line item in the financial statement was an accounting
  classification issue and relates to remuneration for the previous Independent Member of the Administrative Board
  under article 19 par. 6 of the PRAGMA Statutes.

  The Administrative Board recommends the approval of the financial statement by the General Assembly.

  **Decision:** The General Assembly unanimously approves the Financial Statement 2025.

  ### b. Budget for Next Financial Year, 2026

  Discussed together with item c. below.

  ### c. PRAGMA Membership Fee for Next Financial Year, 2026

  The membership has received PRAGMA's proposed budget for 2026. The Administrative Board recommends approval of the
  budget for 2026 with clarifications on recurring costs, legal and intellectual property contingencies, and the
  relationship between projected expenses and membership fees.

  The Administrative Board notes that membership fees alone may not be sufficient to cover all running costs if all
  budgeted contingency items are needed. The discussion included infrastructure costs currently paid directly by a
  member on PRAGMA's behalf. The Administrative Board noted that PRAGMA should either pay such costs directly,
  reimburse the member, or document the arrangement appropriately for governance purposes.

  Members discussed monthly installment payments of the membership fee and the need to preserve flexibility for
  potential trademark disputes and new PRAGMA projects. The members therefore suggest paying the membership fees in
  monthly installments and reassessing PRAGMA's financial needs for the final two quarters of 2026. If certain
  budgeted items are not needed, based on actual projects received and the status of potential disputes, the General
  Assembly may reassess the need for full payment of outstanding installments.

  **Decision:** The General Assembly unanimously approves the membership fees and budget for 2026, implementing:

  - monthly installment payments; and
  - a reassessment of the requirement to pay outstanding installments in late 2026 if other budget items are not
  needed.
