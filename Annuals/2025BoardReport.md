# PRAGMA Administrative Board
## Annual Report 2025

---

## 1. Executive Summary

In 2025, PRAGMA focused on governance consolidation, board continuity and preparing the organization to scale in 2026. Across the year, the Board formalized core policy frameworks (including voting, project reporting, licensing, maintainer committee operations, post-mortem guidance, and incident resolution), established recurring reporting practices and converted General Assembly discussions into actionable programs.

The organization also advanced ecosystem-facing work by launching open coding/mentorship sessions, electing a Project Committee for Phase 2 onboarding, and strengthening engagement with ecosystem partners including Intersect.

At the same time, several structural constraints persisted: 2026 dues decisions, ongoing legal and trademark costs, and delayed submission of maintainer formal processes needed for new project onboarding.

## 3. Strategic Program Development

### Administrator/Oversight Scope

The Board evaluated PRAGMA's potential expansion as a formal administrator service provider and participation in the Intersect Oversight Committee. Following review of statutes, budget, and capacity, PRAGMA closed the administrator-service path and declined Oversight Committee participation.

### Project Committee (Phase 2)

A major 2025 outcome was establishment of the Project Committee framework and election of committee members in Q4. This created the operational path for evaluating and onboarding new projects in 2026.

Committee mandate includes:
- Evaluation of prospective projects
- Oversight support post-admission per approved PDRs

### Mentorship & Open Coding

Mentorship was selected as an early, high-impact value proposition and moved from concept to execution in 2025:
- Open coding sessions launched with Maintainer Committees
- Participation expanded to GA members
- Sessions were scheduled and promoted publicly
- Program paused seasonally in late Q4 and planned to resume in January 2026

## 7. Risks, Constraints, and Lessons Learned

### Key Constraints Identified in 2025

- Trademark and legal obligations impose recurring cost pressure.
- Lack of collected 2025 dues limited budget flexibility.
- Volunteer reliance creates execution risk for mentorship and onboarding programs.
- Delayed maintainer process submissions can block onboarding readiness.

### Operating Lessons

- Governance structure and templates materially improved clarity and consistency.
- Programs move fastest when scoped narrowly and tied to clear ownership.
- Public reporting cadence strengthens accountability but requires sustained discipline across committees.

---

## 8. 2026 Priorities

Primary carry-forward priorities are:
1. Finalize and approve 2026 dues model.
2. Complete remaining bank authority and legal continuity actions.
3. Launch Project Committee application and onboarding workflow.
4. Collect and approve Maintainer Committee formal processes.
5. Resume and expand mentorship/open coding sessions on a predictable cadence.
6. Resolve outstanding trademark monitoring and legal retainer decisions.

---

## 9. Closing Statement

2025 was a foundational year for PRAGMA: core governance systems were built, board continuity was maintained through transitions, and Phase 2 structures were established for growth. The organization enters 2026 with stronger policy alignment and clearer execution pathways, while still requiring focused follow-through on financial, legal, and maintainer-readiness dependencies.
