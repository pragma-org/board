## Agenda

### 1. Project Committee Update

The board received an update from the secretary, who attended the most recent project committee meeting. The committee resolved to wait two additional weeks before onboarding the project that had already signed up, with the intention of giving more time for further applications to come in. The general view was that it is preferable to onboard one project carefully and verify that the process functions well rather than onboarding too many at once. The committee has resumed its regular biweekly meeting cadence. The board was encouraged to share the application extension announcement on social media to support broader awareness.

### 2. Maintainer Committee and Legal Services Update

The board noted that, following the Annual General Assembly, the initiative to define a legal services framework for maintainer committees will require further work. This includes setting out the conditions under which PRAGMA would facilitate access to legal support and how such services would be funded. The board noted this as an ongoing initiative. The General Assembly's preference for Bitwarden as the shared credential manager was also noted, with the board to follow up on implementation details.

### 3. Accounting Update

The board discussed several accounting matters arising from the Annual General Assembly and ongoing operations.

**PostFinance – Power of Attorney:** The board was informed that PostFinance requires signed copies of the relevant meeting minutes from the December 2025 extraordinary General Assembly in order to formally establish signatory authority. Two board members were asked to print, sign, and scan the documents and return them promptly.

**Membership Invoices:** The board confirmed that the first batch of membership invoices will be prepared within the coming week, reflecting the approved annual fee of CHF 8,900 divided into twelve monthly instalments.

**VAT Correction:** In line with the General Assembly's approval, the previous membership invoices that erroneously included VAT will be corrected and reissued. No additional payment is required from member organisations.

**Invoice Template:** The board noted that the standard PRAGMA invoice template should be located and provided to the treasurer to ensure invoices are issued in the correct format.

**Accountants' Invoice:** The external accountants' invoice will be entered into PostFinance in the coming days.

### 4. Legal Update

The board received a brief update on the trademark opposition matter. No new information has been received from legal counsel. The board took no action and agreed to wait for an update before proceeding further.

### 5. Open Coding Sessions – Format and Categories

The board discussed the outstanding action item regarding open coding session format categories. A board member confirmed that a session with one General Assembly representative has been scheduled for the current month, and a commitment has been obtained from a second for the following month. The board agreed that the relevant General Assembly representative should be contacted directly to ask for suggested categories so the format can be finalised and implemented.

### 6. PRAGMA Board Discord Server – Decommission

The board received confirmation from legal counsel that there is no legal obligation requiring a permanently tracked communication channel. The board agreed to proceed with decommissioning the PRAGMA board Discord server. Prior to deletion, all content is to be saved and exported to the relevant shared folder.

### 7. Board Chair and Secretary – Annual Elections

The board noted that the PRAGMA statutes require annual elections for both the chair and secretary positions. The board discussed the current chair's performance and the challenges involved in the role, including the difficulty of aligning a dispersed General Assembly and project committee under current ecosystem conditions. All board members expressed appreciation and support for the work carried out.

The board agreed to defer the formal vote to the next monthly meeting, allowing additional time to consider how to better structure support for whoever holds the chair going forward. The incumbent secretary confirmed willingness to continue in the role. A formal vote on both positions will take place at the June meeting.

### 8. Pull Request Review

The board was reminded that several pull requests in the board repository remain open and require review and approval, including quarterly reports, the annual report, and meeting minutes. All board members were asked to review and approve the relevant pull requests.

### 9. Project Committee Oversight

The board agreed that the secretary will take on a more active oversight role within the project committee, including helping to manage the onboarding cadence and committee activities. This was welcomed as a way to reduce the workload on the board chair and improve coordination within the committee.

### 10. Cardano Foundation .ADA / .Cardano Top-Level Domain Application

The board considered a request for PRAGMA to sign a letter of support for the Cardano Foundation's application to ICANN for the `.ada` and `.cardano` top-level domains. The background to the initiative was shared, including the significant cost and lengthy timeline involved in ICANN applications and the value of ecosystem support in strengthening the application. A potential conflict of interest was disclosed by one board member, who noted a related commercial initiative at their organisation, but did not consider this a reason to oppose the letter.

The board discussed the initiative and its potential benefit to the Cardano ecosystem. All board members expressed agreement to sign the letter of support. The chair confirmed she would print, sign, and return the letter, and the treasurer confirmed he would circulate the document for signature.

**Resolution:** The board agreed unanimously to sign the letter of support for the Cardano Foundation's ICANN .ada / .cardano top-level domain application.

### 11. Next Meeting

The board noted that the next regular monthly meeting will take place in June 2026.
