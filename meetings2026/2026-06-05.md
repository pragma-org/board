### 3. Maintainer Committees

#### 3a. Legal Services Framework – Probst Partner Engagement

The board discussed the initiative to define a legal services framework for maintainer committees, as agreed at the General Assembly. A quote was received from Probst Partner Partner for drafting the initial regulations document.

The board discussed whether this work should be handled internally or externally. A board member noted willingness to draft an initial version internally to reduce cost, though acknowledged it would be more efficient for Probst Partner to produce the first draft. The board agreed that having the external lawyers produce the first draft and then iterating internally was the preferred approach, given that the current cash position can support it.

> **Resolution:** The board voted unanimously to engage Probst Partner to draft the initial maintainer committee legal services framework.

### 5. Legal Update – Trademark Opposition Resolved

The board received an update on the PRAGMA trademark opposition. The settlement agreement with the opposing party has been signed and is now in execution. The following has been confirmed:

- The opposing party has withdrawn their oppositions in the EU and UK.
- No withdrawal was required in the US, as the opposing party had not submitted a filing there.
- In Singapore, PRAGMA has received the grant of the mark.
- Final formal steps remain pending with WIPO (the international trademark authority), including receipt of official grant confirmations from several national offices. These costs are to be covered by the Cardano Foundation, as previously agreed.

The board noted this as a significant milestone and expects a final update from legal counsel once all formal steps are complete.
