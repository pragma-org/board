## Agenda

### 1. Phase 2 — Project Committee

The board received an update on the Project Committee. The committee's scheduled meeting earlier in the week did not take place, as the committee secretary was unavailable.

- One project application has been reviewed and approved. The committee voted unanimously in favour.
- A second application is currently open for voting.
- Further applications have been received and are under discussion.

The board noted that the committee is now processing applications, following the extended application window agreed at the previous meeting.

### 2. Maintainer Committee

The board received a clarification on the scope of the engagement with Probst Partner, approved at the previous meeting. The board had understood the work to cover two separate documents; counsel intends to deliver a single combined document covering both:

- the legal services framework available to maintainer committees, setting out what support may be requested directly and what requires board approval
- a revision of the existing Amaru contributing and legal document, which is currently lengthy and heavily legal in character.

As the board had already approved the expenditure, the chair confirmed the combined approach to counsel. A first draft is expected within approximately two weeks, subject to counsel's availability.

### 3. Credential Management

No update was available. The board member responsible was not present. The chair will obtain the update separately and report back.

### 4. Financials Update

**Membership Invoices:** The monthly and annual membership invoices have been issued to all members. Board members were asked to confirm receipt within their respective organisations. 

**Corrected VAT Invoices:** The corrected VAT invoices have not yet been issued. The treasurer noted that these are deliberately being held back so that they are not confused with the invoices currently outstanding, which could result in duplicate payment. They will be issued separately within the current year.

**PostFinance Authority:** Counsel is preparing a further form for signature. Once executed, PRAGMA will hold the authority to administer its own banking arrangements directly, removing the need to rely on counsel for routine banking administration.

### 5. Legal Updates

**Retainer — revised arrangement:** The treasurer reported on a call held with Probst Partner to review the retainer. Counsel holds an outstanding balance of approximately CHF 10,000 for additional General Assembly work and for the ongoing administrative support around the PostFinance arrangements. The treasurer proposed a clean settlement: pay the outstanding balance, with an attempt to negotiate it down given the volume of administrative work that arose from the original setup, and move to an ongoing retainer of CHF 6,500 per year — the amount originally budgeted.

The revised retainer would principally cover General Assembly support. The treasurer noted the rationale for retaining external support in that area: it provides continuity across changes in board composition, and independence from the board itself. The retainer represents a ceiling rather than a committed spend; any unused balance is refundable or may be rolled forward.

The treasurer offered to circulate the arrangement in writing for review. The board elected to proceed without a written review round.

> **Resolution — revised legal retainer arrangement:**
> The board approved settlement of the outstanding balance with Probst Partner and the move to an ongoing annual retainer of CHF 6,500 covering General Assembly support.

### 7. PRAGMA's Position in the Ecosystem

The chair reported on a series of conversations held with other ecosystem organisations, covering whether PRAGMA could assist in transitioning or sunsetting open source work into community-owned stewardship, should that become necessary, so that community-funded work is not lost. These conversations remain exploratory.

A board member observed that there is persistent confusion in the community about what PRAGMA is and what operating "under PRAGMA" means, noting that enquiries are received from individuals who assume PRAGMA is an employer. The board acknowledged that it does very little proactive public communication about its role, and discussed whether it should be more visible, including the possibility of hosting a public Space.
