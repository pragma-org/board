### 7. Proposed Board Seats

The board discussed a proposal that PRAGMA take a seat on the board of an ecosystem governance organisation, and separately that PRAGMA join a second, less formal ecosystem body. The seat in question is an appointed rather than an elected seat, carries full voting rights, and a third party has offered to fund it. The board discussed the fee attaching to the seat and considered it disproportionate; the offering party has indicated that reducing it is among its own objectives for the year, and has agreed to fund a single year, after which the arrangement is to be reassessed.

**Arguments in favour:** The board considered that having an open source and infrastructure voice present where ecosystem decisions are actually made would benefit both PRAGMA's mission and the wider ecosystem, particularly as the organisation in question restructures. It was noted that the relevant bodies already carry representation from both major ecosystem organisations, so PRAGMA's presence would not tilt the balance toward either.

**Reservations raised:** Several board members questioned whether additional committee participation produces measurable results, noting that the time cost is high and the impact arguably marginal. On the second body, the board noted it has no formal structure, its future is uncertain, and the commitment required is unpredictable — described as sporadic and difficult to scope. The board seat, by contrast, was considered the clearer of the two propositions.

**Conflicts disclosed:** Two board members disclosed conflicts of interest — one arising from their employment, and one arising from a commercial relationship between their organisation and the organisation concerned. Both were surfaced voluntarily and neither was considered a direct conflict.

**Proposed approach:** The board converged on treating the seat as a time-limited experiment: take it for one year, define in advance what meaningful impact would look like in a small number of measurable terms, and evaluate against that at the end of the term rather than renewing by default. The board did not reach a formal resolution at this meeting.
