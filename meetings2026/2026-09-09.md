### 2. Maintainer Committee Regulations

The chair presented an eight-page draft from Lara Elliott regarding Maintainer Committee regulations for board review. The board discussed the need for the regulations to clarify the legal and practical relationship between PRAGMA, Maintainer Committees and third-party service providers.

Dan raised concerns regarding the lack of separate legal personality for Maintainer Committees when interacting with outside vendors, including potential issues around signing contracts.

Federico highlighted prior ambiguity regarding liability and how mistakes by a Maintainer Committee could affect PRAGMA, including the Administrative Board.

The board noted that the regulations should help clarify responsibility, liability and operational boundaries. The discussion also included the need to keep project funding separate from PRAGMA funds, including where funding is administered through ecosystem mechanisms such as Intersect.

The board will review the draft and provide feedback async.
