## Agenda

### 1. Phase 2 — Project Committee

The board received an update on the Project Committee. Two applications have been approved and a third, for a contracts library, remains under discussion. 

**Composition:** The committee chair has stepped down. With the committee secretary already unavailable, the committee has neither a chair nor a secretary. The three remaining members will be asked directly whether they intend to continue. 
**Communication with the applicants:**  the board secretary will take over one relationship and the chair retains the other.



Key points discussed:

- The committee was given a broad remit without sufficient definition of what was expected. Clearer guidelines and a role description, including the expected commitment of around two hours per month, are required.
- A board member suggested some members may have sought the position for standing in the ecosystem rather than for the work, in which case a resignation and a fresh call for members would be preferable.
- The board was cautioned against treating payment as the remedy before the cause is established, and noted that in-kind recognition is an alternative to direct payment.
- Volunteer-engagement experience shared with the board indicated that the primary motivator is a sense of making a difference. A board member will attend several committee meetings and report observations back.

**Compensation and external funding:** The board confirmed that compensating committee members is permissible, but was unwilling to fund it from the Cardano treasury or from increased membership dues. 
It reviewed external funding options identified by the secretary and supported pursuing an application. Where compensation is introduced, it must be matched by a defined role description, performance expectations, and removal of members who do not meet them.

**Recruitment:** No structural changes will be made before the scheduled January elections. The secretary has compiled a list of prospective members identified from public development activity and will vet and approach suitable candidates. At least two seats are expected to require filling.

### 2. Proposed New Project — Node Performance Comparison Page

The board was informed of a request to recognise a node performance comparison and conformance-testing webpage as an official PRAGMA project, separated from an existing project, together with a request to use the PRAGMA mark. No maintaining team was confirmed and the request to use the mark was withdrawn.

- The board was not prepared to attach the PRAGMA identity to work without an identified maintainer.
- A board member held that any such proposal should go through the established onboarding process in full, however expedited, as departing from it would undermine PRAGMA's own rules.
- The board noted that repositories are created in the PRAGMA GitHub organisation with some regularity and could be mistaken for projects.

The board agreed to take no action and to await developments.

### 3. Maintainer Committee

An update was provided on the Maintainer Committee regulation prepared by corporate counsel. Following a meeting with counsel in the past week the document is considered substantially settled; the annex file and a detailed review remain outstanding. Board members who raised liability concerns at the previous meeting have not yet completed their review and will provide feedback asynchronously.

The purpose of the regulation is to segregate the projects from the association as far as is practicable, and to give maintainer committees a framework to present to external counterparties.

### 4. Financials Update

An update was provided on PRAGMA's financial position.

- The balance stands at approximately CHF 10,000, following settlement of roughly CHF 10,000 in legal fees largely attributable to the banking transition.
- Upcoming commitments include the CHF 7,000 retainer, an outstanding tax payment, and the year-end accounting close.
- Payment of membership fees by instalment creates a liquidity constraint. Members were asked to pay on time where possible and to raise any difficulty with the treasurer.
- One member organisation has never paid its fees, representing CHF 8,900 against a budget that assumed payment. The chair will approach that member; the treasurer will separately ask a General Assembly delegate what terms were agreed.
- The registered postal address has not yet been circulated, pending confirmation of the required format and of mail-handling arrangements. This remains a standing monthly item.
- 2026 accounting invoices will be uploaded to the shared folder.

### 5. Legal Updates

#### 5.1 Legal support for Maintainer Committees

The revised retainer with Probst Partner reduced PRAGMA's prepaid cost but removed the allowance for maintainer committees to raise legal questions, which the board and the General Assembly have understood to be part of what PRAGMA offers its projects.

The retainer is a prepayment and additional work may still be commissioned separately. The treasurer will ask the firm whether a discounted or defined hourly rate can be obtained. Support will be iterative and conditioned on each question: simple matters may be handled internally, while anything substantial should be referred to external counsel, both for quality and for liability reasons.

#### 5.2 Liability

A board member raised that PRAGMA paying for legal advice on which a project then acts creates an evident connection between the two. The board noted that under Swiss law the association carries limited liability, that board members are not personally liable other than in narrow edge cases, and that the residual risk is low given PRAGMA’s size and character. Attribution between projects and PRAGMA cannot be eliminated; the regulation seeks to segregate the two as far as possible rather than remove the risk.

The board agreed to obtain estimates for directors’ and officers’ insurance for consideration in next year’s budget. 

### 6. CIP-113 Governance Framework

An update was provided on CIP-113. PRAGMA has been invited to join a federated group stewarding the standard, amounting to one signing position for upgrades and an opinion on proposed improvements. The board considered the matter primarily technical and better suited to the General Assembly, which must also be free to decline the role. No decision is required at this stage; the governance framework will be provided when prepared.
### 7. 2027 Ecosystem Budget — Domain Stewardship

The board was briefed on a drafted process for the 2027 ecosystem budget, which would replace the existing funding categories with seven domains, six of them under an institutional steward. PRAGMA has been proposed as steward for the developer support domain.

The board is willing to consider the role and will form a position once the proposal is published and the community response is visible.
