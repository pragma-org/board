# PRAGMA Administrative Board - Quarterly Report
## Q2 2026 (April-June)

---

## 1. Executive Summary

Q2 2026 focused on completing the Annual General Assembly, moving from planning into implementation for several
approved operational items and continued support for the Project Committee.

The General Assembly approved the 2025 financial statement and 2026 budget, confirmed the annual
membership fee with monthly installment options, supported the continuation of PRAGMA's value proposition, approved
the Bitwarden credential management solution and approved the provision of legal services to maintainer committee
members through PRAGMA. Following the General Assembly, the board began implementation work on invoicing, credential
management, maintainer committee legal-services regulations and continued project onboarding.

The quarter also included the annual election of the Administrative Board Chair and Secretary, resolution of the
PRAGMA trademark opposition and a unanimous board decision to support the Cardano Foundation's `.ada` and
`.cardano` ICANN top-level domain application.

## 3. General Assembly

The 2026 Ordinary General Assembly was held on 6 May 2026. Key outcomes included:

- approval of the continuation of PRAGMA's value proposition;
- acknowledgement that implementation began in 2025 for open-source mentorship and field-tested documentation and
  workflows;
- acknowledgement that no headway was made in 2025 on open-source project maturity assessment or PRAGMA ecosystem
  recommendations;
- approval for the Administrative Board to proceed with the Bitwarden credential management solution;
- unanimous approval for PRAGMA to provide legal services to maintainer committee members, particularly for
  contract negotiations or disputes with third-party service providers;
- approval for the Administrative Board to draw up and issue the necessary maintainer committee legal-services
  regulations;
- unanimous approval of a correction to past VAT-included membership invoices;
- approval of the 2026 budget and annual membership fee, with monthly installment payments options and a
  late-2026 reassessment of whether outstanding instalments remain necessary if budgeted items are not needed;
- approval of the Administrative Board's planned 2026 activities: finalising dues, further advertising Project
  Committee applications, continuing to expand the mentorship and open coding programme, and developing tools for
  open-source project maturity assessment and ecosystem recommendations.

After the General Assembly, the board began converting these approvals into operational work, including membership
invoicing, Bitwarden setup and the maintainer committee legal-services framework.

## 4. Project Committee (Phase 2)

The Project Committee continued its work during Q2, though the board noted in April that the committee was in a
slower period and no formal update was provided at that meeting.

In May, the secretary reported that the Project Committee had resumed a regular biweekly meeting cadence. The
committee chose to extend the application period before onboarding the first project that
had applied. The board supported the cautious approach of onboarding a few projects carefully and verifying that the
process works before scaling to more.

The board also agreed that the secretary would take a more active oversight role in the Project Committee,
including helping manage onboarding cadence and committee activities. This was intended to improve coordination and
reduce workload pressure on the Chair. Several new projects were in discussion with the Project Committee about 
applying, two of which applied and two more were in discussions.

The board reviewed the PRAGMA goals draft document after it had been circulated for General Assembly feedback.
The board agreed to formally endorse the document and present it to the General Assembly for review and input but
upon learning a previous version existed, the document was archived.

## 5. Maintainer Committees

### Outstanding Reports

The board continued tracking outstanding maintainer committee reporting obligations during Q2. The board
noted that the responsible project lead had communicated significant external demands on his time. The board agreed
to grant additional time for the outstanding reports to be submitted while also confirming the need that more 
discussion needs to be had around maintainer committee leads, obligations and replacement process needs.

### Legal Services Framework

Following the General Assembly's approval for PRAGMA to provide legal services to maintainer committee members, the 
board reviewed a quote from Probst Partner to draft the initial regulations document. Given the importance of the
framework and the available cash position, the board unanimously approved engaging Probst Partner to draft the
initial maintainer committee legal-services framework for internal review and iteration.

### Reusable Maintainer Committee Template

The General Assembly noted that the PRAGMA-Amaru Maintainer Committee framework agreement, developed in 2025 by the
Cardano Foundation legal team, could serve as a basis for the legal-services regulations and as a template
framework agreement for future maintainer committees. The board agreed that this could be simplified and adapted 
into a reusable template for other maintainer committees. This work is included in the Probst Partner quote above.

## 6. Financial & Accounting Updates

The board continued accounting and banking follow-up throughout the quarter.

The board received an update on the ongoing financial review with the external accounting firm. Remaining
questions related to VAT provisions were addressed and the review completed before the Annual General Assembly.

Following the General Assembly:

- the annual membership fee was confirmed;
- membership invoices were sent in June;
- the General Assembly approved a correction for prior membership invoices that had incorrectly included VAT, using
  replacement invoices to remedy the issue without sending money back and forth;
- the VAT correction invoices were sent at the end of June;
- Blink Labs' direct payment of Google Workspace and Cloudflare costs was discussed, with a one-page governance
  note suggested to document Blink Labs' intent to cover those items as a donation to PRAGMA and PRAGMA's use of
  the resources;

In June, the board discussed reducing the Probst Partner legal retainer. The treasurer noted that some services
previously covered by the retainer, including General Assembly preparation and banking coordination, could now be
handled internally because a lawyer had joined the board. The board unanimously authorized the treasurer to
negotiate a reduced retainer and revised scope of services.

The board also discussed the need for an official PRAGMA mailing address. A Zurich office provider offering a
registered address with mail scanning and forwarding was being explored.

## 7. Registry & Credential Management

The board continued work on shared credential management for PRAGMA projects.

In April, the board reviewed pricing for two password-manager options. Given the on going costs of these password
managers, the board opted to present both options to the General Assembly, recommend the lower-cost option.

The General Assembly approved proceeding with Bitwarden, limited to one seat per General Assembly member. A board 
member agreed to take responsibility for setup, including determining the payment method. The board
noted that care should be taken to avoid operational costs defaulting to a member organisation's own accounts.

## 8. Legal & Brand Matters

### Trademark Opposition

In June, the board received confirmation that the settlement agreement had been signed and was in execution. The
opposing party withdrew its oppositions in the EU and UK. No withdrawal was required in the US because the opposing
party had not submitted a filing there. PRAGMA also received the grant of the mark in Singapore.

Final formal steps remain pending with WIPO and several national offices. The associated costs are to be covered by
the Cardano Foundation as previously agreed.

### Maintainer Committee Contract Support

In April, the board noted that a PRAGMA project was in direct contact with legal counsel to review a contract. The
associated costs are to be billed directly to the project's own budget and not to PRAGMA.

### Cardano Foundation .ADA / .Cardano Top-Level Domain Application

In May, the board considered a request for PRAGMA to sign a letter of support for the Cardano Foundation's
application to ICANN for the `.ada` and `.cardano` top-level domains. The board discussed the potential ecosystem
benefit, the cost and timeline of ICANN applications and a disclosed potential conflict of interest related to a
commercial initiative at one board member's organisation.

The board unanimously agreed and signed the letter of support.
