# PRAGMA Administrative Board - Quarterly Report
## Q3 2026 (July-September)

---

## 1. Executive Summary

Q3 2026 focused on moving PRAGMA's Phase 2 Project Committee from preparation into active application processing, advancing the Maintainer Committee regulations and legal-services framework, and clarifying PRAGMA's possible role in the wider Cardano ecosystem.

During the quarter, the Project Committee processed applications and approved the onboarding of `cardano-init` and TapDano. The board also provided temporary cover for the Project Committee secretary and continued discussing how to support open-source initiatives that may need a long-term home.

The board made progress on PRAGMA's legal and operational infrastructure. This included approving a revised Probst Partner retainer arrangement, consolidating the registered mailing address with counsel, progressing the PostFinance authority handover, continuing credential-management negotiations and receiving confirmation that the PRAGMA trademark matters were resolved.

The quarter also included discussion of a possible PRAGMA seat on an ecosystem governance organisation, PRAGMA representation at Token 2049, the redesigned website and additional developer-engagement opportunities. The Maintainer Committee regulations remain under asynchronous board review.

## 4. Project Committee (Phase 2)

The Project Committee moved into active application processing during Q3.

Early in the quarter, the board was informed that one application had been reviewed and approved, a second was open for voting and additional applications were under discussion.

## 5. Maintainer Committees

### Maintainer Committee Regulations

The board received an eight-page draft from Lara Elliott during the quarter. The draft is intended to clarify the legal and practical relationship between PRAGMA, Maintainer Committees and third-party service providers.

Board members were asked to review the draft asynchronously. The regulations also relate to the legal-services framework approved in Q2 and the operating guidelines for Maintainer Committees.

### Probst Partner Retainer and Future Maintainer Committee Support

The main legal-services development was to reduce and restructure PRAGMA's Probst Partner retainer.

The board approved settling the outstanding balance with Probst Partner and moving to an ongoing annual retainer of CHF 6,500 covering General Assembly support. Beni worked to reduce the approximately CHF 10,000 outstanding balance, which included additional General Assembly work and administrative support related to the PostFinance arrangements. The arrangement was being restructured into a reduced package that included mailbox service, while a pending invoice remained open for prior work, including banking handover work. The expectation was that the position would be reset once the new scope was finalised.

## 9. Ecosystem Representation and Governance Participation

### Proposed Ecosystem Governance Seat

The board discussed a proposal for PRAGMA to take an appointed, full-voting seat on the board of an ecosystem governance organisation. 

The board saw potential value in having an open-source and infrastructure voice present during a period of organisational change. Reservations included the time cost, uncertain measurable impact and the risk of taking on an additional commitment without clear boundaries. The board considered a one-year, time-limited experiment with predefined success criteria and an end-of-term evaluation, but did not adopt a formal resolution during the meeting.

Two board members disclosed potential conflicts of interest. The disclosures were made voluntarily and were not considered direct conflicts at the meeting.

### Token 2049

The board discussed PRAGMA representation at Token 2049. Given limits on PRAGMA's participation, the chair proposed sending Pi Lanningham if representation was possible, both to promote PRAGMA and to highlight recent ecosystem DeFi developments. The board agreed with that proposal.

## 11. Governance and Operational Risks Noted

The Q3 records identify several issues requiring continued attention:

- Maintainer Committee legal personality, contracting authority and liability remain insufficiently clear until the regulations are finalised.
- Funding administered through ecosystem mechanisms must remain clearly separated from PRAGMA funds and responsibilities.
- Membership invoice settlement remain outstanding.
- The Probst Partner outstanding balance and revised scope remain to be finalised.
- A possible external governance seat should not become an open-ended commitment without defined success criteria, time limits and conflict management.
- Project Committee onboarding should preserve a consistent and documented application process, including how incomplete or non-conforming submissions are handled.
- PRAGMA's public explanation of its role and stewardship offer remains limited, despite increased interest from ecosystem initiatives.
